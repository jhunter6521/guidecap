Guidecap

Click through a task. Get a step-by-step guide.

Guidecap is a Chrome extension that records your clicks and turns them into a how-to guide with screenshots. It's made for IT how-tos, SOPs and onboarding docs, and your guides never leave your computer.

Why Guidecap

Writes the steps for you

Private by design

No cloud, no account, no upload. Guidecap never records what you type.

Hide sensitive info

Black out passwords, emails and customer data before you share a guide.
